📖 Common Grammar Mistakes

Their / There / They're

Their = possession, There = location, They're = they are.

Your / You're

Your = possession, You're = you are.

Its / It's

Its = possession, It's = it is.

To / Too / Two

To = direction, Too = also or excessive, Two = number.

Then / Than

Then = time, Than = comparison.

Effect / Affect

Effect = result (noun), Affect = influence (verb).

💡 Pro Writing Tips

Read Aloud

Reading your work aloud helps catch awkward phrasing and missing words that your eyes might skip.

Take a Break

Step away from your writing for a few hours or a day. Fresh eyes catch more errors.

Print It Out

Printing your work makes it feel different and helps you spot errors you'd miss on screen.

Use Tools Wisely

Grammar checkers are helpful but don't rely on them completely. Learn the rules yourself.

Read More

The more you read, the more you absorb correct grammar and writing styles naturally.

Peer Review

Have someone else read your work. They'll catch errors you've become blind to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this grammar checker free to use?
Yes! Our grammar checker is completely free. No sign-up required, no limits on usage.
What types of errors does it check?
It checks spelling, punctuation, subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, commonly confused words, and basic grammar rules.
Is my text stored or saved anywhere?
No. Your text is processed locally in your browser and is not stored on any server. Your privacy is fully protected.
Can I use this for academic writing?
Yes! It's perfect for essays, research papers, and assignments. Always do a final proofread yourself as well.
What's the difference between grammar and spelling?
Spelling is about correctly forming words. Grammar is about how words are structured into sentences — including tense, agreement, and word order.
How can I improve my grammar skills?
Read regularly, practice writing, use grammar checkers to learn from your mistakes, and study grammar rules. Consistency is key.
