Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files safe and private?
Yes! All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server. Everything stays on your device.
What file types are supported?
For PDF tools: .pdf files. For conversion: .jpg, .png, .gif, .webp, and .bmp images are supported.
Is there a file size limit?
Since processing is done in your browser, the limit depends on your device's memory. For most students, files up to 50-100 MB work well.
How do I merge PDFs?
Select the "Merge PDF" tool, upload your PDF files (in the order you want them merged), and click "Merge PDFs". Your merged file will be downloaded automatically.
What happens to my files after processing?
Files are processed only in your browser's memory. Once you close the page or refresh, everything is cleared. No data is stored or logged.
Can I use these tools on mobile?
Yes! The tools are fully responsive and work on smartphones and tablets. The interface adapts to your screen size for easy use.
